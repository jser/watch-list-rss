<p><strong>Tool:</strong> <a href="https://tools.simonwillison.net/seam-cutter">Seam Cutter</a></p> <p>I built this weird and very specific cropping tool - it lets you create a horizontal or vertical cut across an image, then slide the two sides relative to each other. Effectively it lets you crop out a section in the middle of an image.</p><p>I wanted this because I often use <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailor-screenshot-stitching/id926653095">Tailor</a> on my iPhone to stitch several screenshots together into lot long image, but sometimes I find myself wanting to crop out a section in the middle - to redact an irrelevant prompt from a transcript, for example.</p> <p>Tags: <a href="https://simonwillison.net/tags/tools">tools</a></p>
